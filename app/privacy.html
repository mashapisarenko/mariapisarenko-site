Privacy

Read Music Notes: Dr. Maria Pisarenko · last updated October 18, 2026

Read Music Notes is a free app for learning to read music. It opens without an account, an email address or any sign-up.

What the app keeps

Your lesson stars, records, practice ticks and settings are saved only in your own browser on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

The microphone

Microphone mode is optional and off until you choose it. When you turn it on, your browser asks for permission. The sound is analyzed on your device to recognize which note you played. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Fonts

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. As with any website that does this, your browser contacts Google's servers to download them. The app sends Google no information about you or your progress.

No advertising, no tracking

The app shows no ads, uses no advertising or analytics trackers, and sells nothing about you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Piano sound

The piano is the Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license.
