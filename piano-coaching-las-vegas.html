Who it is for

One performance that matters

Actors playing a pianist

For screen or stage: how a pianist sits, breathes and moves, where the hands go, and how a real performance of your piece looks — so the camera believes it.

A piece at your own wedding

For the bride, the groom or someone in the family who wants to play one piece for the people in the room — prepared so that it feels like a gift, not an ordeal.

An anniversary or celebration

A piece for a parent, a partner or a friend, played on the day. We choose something that suits your hands and your time, and build it to the date.

Auditions, recitals, competitions

Preparation for a single high-stakes performance: the programme, the details that examiners and juries hear, and playing under pressure.

A recording or video

Getting one piece ready for the camera or the microphone, where every take is heard again.

Regular lessons instead?

If you want to learn the piano over time, that is Las Vegas Piano School, or Online Piano University from anywhere.

How it works

Planned backwards from your date

  • Tell me the piece, the date and where it will happen. If you do not have a piece yet, we choose one together that suits the moment and the time you have.
  • We plan backwards from the date, so the piece is ready with room to spare rather than on the morning itself.
  • In person in Las Vegas, or online — whichever fits your schedule and wherever you are.
  • For actors, we work from the scene: what the camera sees, what the audience hears, and what has to be real.
